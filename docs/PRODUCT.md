# Tvium Product Definition

## Mission

Tvium transforms user-provided IPTV sources into a fast, understandable, and elegant media library. Its product test is simple: every feature must make channels, films, and series easier to find, organize, understand, or watch.

## Product promise

Tvium is a reader and library manager. It provides no channel, playlist, subscription, provider access, proprietary catalogue, or recommendation of IPTV providers. Users supply their own M3U file/URL or Xtream Codes profile and remain responsible for their sources and rights.

The first release is free and ad-free, with no Tvium account, paywall, artificial library limit, required tracking, or premium payment. A discreet, voluntary support link may be added later without unlocking functionality.

## Experience principles

1. **Library before raw playlist:** preserve the source while presenting a clearer local organization.
2. **Local first:** credentials, preferences, history, and watch progress stay on the device by default.
3. **Fast at scale:** 10,000–100,000 entries must not block the interface or create thousands of widgets.
4. **Platform appropriate:** phone, tablet, desktop, and TV use purpose-built density, navigation, and player layouts.
5. **Honest compatibility:** codec, network, CORS, and platform limits are reported rather than hidden.
6. **Safe by default:** credentials and stream URLs never enter logs, analytics, or crash payloads.

## Primary journeys

- Add and analyze an M3U source or Xtream profile.
- Review counts and import the source into a local library.
- Browse Live TV, Movies, and TV Shows from a single home hierarchy.
- Search, organize, hide, favorite, and group large libraries.
- Play media with reliable recovery and platform-appropriate controls.

Phase 0 validates the technical foundations of these journeys; it is not a production release.
