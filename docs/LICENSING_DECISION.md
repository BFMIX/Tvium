# Licensing Decision

No license is selected during Phase 0. Generated package-template license files are removed so they cannot accidentally grant inconsistent rights.

Before publishing the repository, the owner should decide whether Tvium is open source, source-available, or proprietary. The review must include:

- desired permissions for use, modification, redistribution, and commercial forks;
- attribution and patent terms;
- copyleft compatibility expectations;
- licenses and distribution obligations of playback engines/native binaries;
- App Store terms and trademark/branding protection.

Permissive candidates such as Apache-2.0 or MPL-2.0 may be evaluated, but no candidate is endorsed yet. Until a root `LICENSE` is deliberately added, normal copyright restrictions apply.
