# Store Guidelines

Store listings must call Tvium an IPTV/media player for user-provided sources. They must not imply bundled channels, provider partnerships, guaranteed format compatibility, access to paid media, or evasion of geographic/technical restrictions.

Required listing themes:

- “Add your own M3U playlist or compatible profile.”
- “Tvium does not provide channels, playlists, or subscriptions.”
- Local-first organization and privacy.
- Platform-specific compatibility limitations.

Screenshots use fictional demo names and authorized images. Do not show third-party channel logos or recognizable copyrighted catalogue art without permission. Support links remain discreet and do not unlock features.

Before submission, review Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Web distribution rules current at that time; this preparatory document is not a substitute for their policies.
