# Legal Position

Tvium is media-player software. It does not provide, sell, host, recommend, or distribute channels, subscriptions, IPTV providers, playlists, or media catalogues.

Users add their own sources and are responsible for ensuring they have the rights and authorization required to access and play their content. Tvium does not validate ownership and this statement is not legal advice.

Product and Store text must maintain this separation. Promotional screenshots use public-domain, freely licensed, fictional, or explicitly authorized material. Channel logos, programme art, and provider marks are not used in marketing without permission.

Legal text requires qualified review before public release and must be adapted to each Store and distribution jurisdiction.
