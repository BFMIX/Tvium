## Product Name

Tvium

## Subtitle

Your IPTV, beautifully organized.

## Short Description

Play and organize your own IPTV sources across phones, computers, browsers, TVs, and streaming boxes.

## Full Description

Tvium brings your own live channels, movies, and TV shows together in one clear, cinematic library. Add an M3U playlist or connect a compatible Xtream source, then browse content by category, switch between household profiles, find favorites, and return to recently played titles.

The app is designed for remote controls, touch screens, keyboards, and large displays. Programme guide data can be added from an XMLTV source, while optional metadata improves presentation without replacing the original playlist information.

Tvium does not provide channels, playlists, subscriptions, or media. Users must supply sources they are authorized to access. Availability and playback compatibility depend on the source, device, codec, network, and regional rules.

## Feature Highlights

- Live TV, movies, and TV shows in one library.
- M3U file, M3U URL, and compatible Xtream source support.
- Household profiles with shared sources and separate favorites, history, and parental settings.
- XMLTV programme guide with Now and Next information.
- Local-first organization with no required Tvium account.
